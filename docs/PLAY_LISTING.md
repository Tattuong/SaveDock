## Full description

SaveDock is for the link, guide, or file you would otherwise send to yourself. Share it in, or tap Save. It lands in Unsorted. Then mark it Read, Try, Buy, or Reference, and set a reminder.

Swipe a card when you are done. Group saves into a collection. Search by name, note, site, or type. Export the library as a backup. Everything stays on this device. Pages are not downloaded for offline reading.

Looks, keep-screen-on, tap sound, and SaveDock Pro (more than 8 saves) unlock with coins from the daily reward or 10 coin packs. Remove ads and SaveDock Pro are one-time unlocks.
