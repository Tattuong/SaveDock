Privacy Policy – SaveDock

Last updated: October 2026

SaveDock keeps links, notes, files, and reminders on this phone. We do not create an account or upload your dock.

Data stored on device

  • Saved links, notes, files, lanes, reminders, and collections
  • Coins, owned shop items, and the active look
  • Purchase tokens needed to restore Remove ads and SaveDock Pro

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing – coin packs, Remove ads, and SaveDock Pro (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – GET https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N315.json to read the disable field (no personal data is sent)

Opening a link leaves the app for the site you saved. SaveDock does not download pages for offline reading. We do not sell personal data. Contact the developer through the Play Store listing.
